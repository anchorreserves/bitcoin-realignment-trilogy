## Overview & User Guide

This eight-tab empirical protocol tracks the progressive neutralization of the four fiat mechanisms (fractional-reserve lending, Cantillon effects, K-shaped divergence, and buy-borrow-die incentives) as Bitcoin adoption advances. The four tests (A.1–A.4) directly evaluate the structural obsolescence of Keynesian demand-management tools. Methods and CHANGELOG sheets accompany the eight-tab workbook and record reconstruction rules and cell-level vintage edits.

**The Four Tests and Their Correspondence to the Trilogy**  
- **A.1 Praxeological Test** → Section 1 of all three papers  
- **A.2 Transmission-Mechanism Metrics** → Section 2 of the Dismantlement Framework & Capstone  
- **A.3 Historical Episode Review** → Section 3 of the Capstone  
- **A.4 Forward Metric Tracking** → Section 3 of the Dismantlement Framework & Section 5 of the Capstone  

**How to Update (Quarterly Cadence)**  
This appendix follows the eight-tab workbook protocol (Overview, Dashboard, A.1, A.2, A.3, A.4, Resources, Schedule & Milestones), together with the Methods and CHANGELOG sheets.  
1. Mark row “In Progress” in the Schedule tab.  
2. Update Tabs 3–6 with latest data.  
3. Dashboard and Realignment Index update automatically.  
4. Record reconstruction notes in Methods and cell-level edits in CHANGELOG as needed.  
5. Mark row “Complete” and add notes.
