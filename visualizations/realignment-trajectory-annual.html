The Realignment Trilogy Framework Projection

This chart presents a mathematical projection of the transition rate from fiat-based monetary mechanisms to Bitcoin-protocol structures, based on the empirical tests and forward metrics defined in the Realignment Trilogy and Anchor Reserves quarterly log data (through 15 June 2026).

The projection illustrates the progressive neutralization of key fiat dynamics (elastic credit creation, Cantillon effects, K-shaped divergence, and discretionary policy transmission) using observed rates from the research. The timeline is determined solely by the calibrated growth and decay functions; no fixed endpoint was imposed.

How to read the chart and table:
• Blue line = projected global Bitcoin adoption
• Green line = self-custody ratio
• Red line = Realignment Index (lower values indicate greater neutralization of fiat mechanisms)
• Shaded area = confidence intervals reflecting increasing uncertainty over longer horizons

The table provides annual values for all variables and the corresponding status category derived from the four-test protocol.
